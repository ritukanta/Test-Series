General Instructions & Examination Scheme
  1. Total Duration: The total duration of this examination is 60 minutes (1 hour). A strict countdown clock is displayed on the top right corner of the screen.
  2. Automatic Submission: Once the 60 minutes countdown completes, the examination will automatically submit your responses.
  3. Marking Scheme: Each correct answer awards +2.00 marks. Each incorrect answer incurs a penalty of -0.50 marks (negative marking). Unattempted questions carry 0 marks.
  4. Option Randomization: The order of four distractor options for each question is automatically randomized upon every page refresh to evaluate authentic mastery, while question order remains strictly sequential (Q.1 to Q.100).
  5. Single Question View: One question is displayed at a time on the screen along with its corresponding Reading Comprehension passage. You can freely navigate to any question at any time.
Question Palette Legend & Status Indicators

The question palette displayed on the right side of the screen shows the status of each question using the following colors:

1 Not Visited: You have not visited the question yet.
2 Not Answered: You have visited the question but have not answered it.
3 Answered: You have answered the question.
4 Marked for Review: You have NOT answered the question, but have marked it for review.
5 Answered & Marked for Review: The question is answered and also marked for review (will be evaluated).
Navigating and Answering Questions
  • Click on the desired option (radio button or option card) to select your answer. Selecting an option will NOT jump to the next question automatically.
  • Click Save & Next to save your answer and navigate to the next question.
  • Click Mark for Review & Next to save the answer (if selected) or mark it for review, and proceed to the next question.
  • Click Clear Response to deselect the selected option for the current question.
  • Click Previous to return to the preceding question without losing your saved responses.
  • You can click any question number on the independent scrollable palette on the right to jump directly to it.
  • To preserve exam integrity, clicking Pause stops the timer and temporarily hides the question view until you resume.
