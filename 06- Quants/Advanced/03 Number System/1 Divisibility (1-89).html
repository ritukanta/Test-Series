Please read the instructions carefully before starting the examination:

  1. The clock will be set at the server. The countdown timer in the top right corner of screen will display the remaining time available for you to complete the examination (89 Minutes for 89 Questions). When the timer reaches zero, the examination will end by itself.
  2. The Question Palette displayed on the right side of the screen (on desktop/tablet) will show the status of each question using one of the following symbols:
1 You have not visited the question yet.
2 You have not answered the question.
3 You have answered the question.
4 Marked for review (not answered).
5 Answered & Marked for review (will be evaluated).

Navigating & Answering a Question:

  • Click on the question number in the Question Palette at the right of your screen to go to that numbered question directly. Note that using this option does NOT automatically save your answer to the current question unless an option is selected.
  • Select one of the four radio button options to choose your answer. Selecting an option does NOT automatically jump to the next question.
  • Use Next or Previous to move across questions, Clear to deselect your chosen option, and Mark for Review & Next to flag a question for review.
  • Upon clicking Start Mock Test, the exam will automatically enter Fullscreen mode. You can pause the test or exit fullscreen at any time using the header controls.
