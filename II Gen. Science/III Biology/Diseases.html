Marking Scheme
  1. Each question carries +2 marks for a correct answer and -0.5 marks for an incorrect answer. Unattempted questions carry 0 marks.
  2. Questions remain in fixed sequence across refreshes, while the four answer options for each question are randomized automatically.
  3. Selecting an option does not reveal the answer or jump to the next question automatically. Use Next, Previous, Clear, or Mark for Review & Next.
  4. The countdown timer runs strictly at 36 seconds per question (42:00 total). The test submits automatically when the timer reaches 00:00.
  5. Clicking Start Test enters full-screen mode automatically. You may pause the test or exit full-screen at any time.
  6. After submission, your score, accuracy, and detailed solutions for incorrect and unattempted questions only will be displayed and saved in your browser.
