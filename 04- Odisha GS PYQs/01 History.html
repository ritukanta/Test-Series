General Instructions:

  1. The clock will be set at the server. The strict countdown timer in the top right corner of the screen will display the remaining time available for you to complete the examination (37 minutes for 37 questions). When the timer reaches zero, the examination will end and submit by itself.
  2. One Multiple Choice Question (MCQ) with four options is displayed on the screen at a time. The screen will not jump to the next question automatically upon selecting an option.
  3. No answers, hints, or scores are revealed during the test. Your final score and detailed analysis are displayed only upon test submission.
  4. Upon submission, solutions and correct answers are provided exclusively for incorrect and un-attempted questions for focused revision.
  5. The Question Number Palette displayed on the right side of the desktop screen has independent scrolling so that main navigation buttons remain visible at all times. Use the palette symbols below to track question status:
Symbol Meaning of Symbol in Question Palette
1 You have not visited the question yet.
2 You have visited the question but have not answered it.
3 You have answered the question.
4 You have NOT answered the question, but have marked the question for review.
5 You have answered the question and marked it for review (will be evaluated for scoring).

Navigating & Answering a Question:

  • Click on an option to select your answer. To deselect your chosen answer, click on the Clear button.
  • Click on Next to save your current selection and move to the next question, or Previous to go back to the preceding question.
  • Click on Mark for Review & Next to flag the current question for review and move to the next question.
  • The test will enter Fullscreen mode automatically upon starting. You can exit fullscreen anytime using the Exit Fullscreen button in the top bar.
