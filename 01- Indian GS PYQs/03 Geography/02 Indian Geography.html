General Examination Instructions

  • The total duration of this chapter mock test is strictly 58 minutes (1 minute per question).
  • The countdown timer in the top right corner of the screen displays the remaining time. When the timer reaches zero, the examination will submit automatically.
  • One Multiple Choice Question (MCQ) is displayed on the screen at a time with four options. Options are randomized on every load/attempt while the question sequence remains fixed.
  • Selecting an option does not reveal the answer during the test and does not jump to the next question automatically.
  • Upon starting the test, the browser will automatically enter Fullscreen mode. You can exit fullscreen at any time using the Exit Fullscreen button.
  • Upon submission, your score and detailed solutions will be displayed exclusively for incorrect and un-attempted questions.

Question Palette Symbols (Right Panel on Desktop)

1 You have not visited the question yet.
2 You have visited but not answered the question.
3 You have answered the question.
4 You have NOT answered the question, but marked it for review.
5 You have answered the question AND marked it for review (will be evaluated on submission).

Navigating & Answering Questions

  • Click on an option to select your response. To deselect your chosen answer, click on the Clear button.
  • Click Next to save your current response and advance to the next question, or Previous to go back to the preceding question.
  • Click Mark for Review & Next to flag the current question for review and move to the next question.
  • Use the scrollable Question Palette on the right side to jump directly to any question number without scrolling the main window.
