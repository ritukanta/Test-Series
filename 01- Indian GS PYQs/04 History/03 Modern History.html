General Exam Instructions (TCS iON / Eduquity Standard)

  1. The total duration of this mock test is 40 minutes (40 questions — 1 minute per question).
  2. The countdown timer in the top right corner of the screen will display the remaining time available for you to complete the examination. When the timer reaches zero, the examination will submit automatically.
  3. One Multiple Choice Question (MCQ) with four options is displayed on the screen at a time. Options are randomized on every attempt, while question numbering remains in strict order.
  4. Selecting an option does not reveal the answer during the test and does not jump to the next question automatically. Use Next, Previous, or Mark for Review & Next to navigate.
  5. The Question Palette displayed on the right side of the screen (on desktop/tablet) shows the status of each question using the following standard symbols:
Symbol Status Meaning
1 You have not visited the question yet.
2 You have visited the question but have not answered it.
3 You have answered the question.
4 You have NOT answered the question, but have marked the question for review.
5 You have answered the question and marked it for review (will be evaluated for score calculation).

Navigating & Answering a Question

  1. Click on the question number in the Question Palette at the right of your screen to go to that numbered question directly. Note that using this option does NOT save your answer to the current question unless an option is already selected.
  2. Click on Next to save your selected response for the current question and move to the next question.
  3. Click on Mark for Review & Next to mark the current question for review (with or without an answer selected) and proceed to the next question.
  4. Click on Clear to deselect your chosen answer for the current question.
  5. Upon submission, your score analysis will be displayed along with solutions/answers exclusively for incorrect and un-attempted questions.
