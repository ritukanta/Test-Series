1. Test Rules & Timing:

  • The countdown timer in the top right corner of the screen displays the remaining time available for you to complete the examination. When the timer reaches zero, the examination will submit automatically.
  • One Multiple Choice Question (MCQ) is displayed on the screen at a time with four options. Selecting an option does not reveal the answer during the test and does not automatically jump to the next question.
  • Upon clicking Start Mock Test, the browser will automatically enter Fullscreen mode. You can exit fullscreen at any time using the Exit Fullscreen button on the top bar.
  • You can pause the countdown timer if required using the Pause button in the header.

2. Question Number Palette Symbols (Right Panel on Desktop):

1 You have not visited the question yet.
2 You have not answered the question.
3 You have answered the question.
4 You have NOT answered the question, but marked it for review.
5 You have answered the question and marked it for review (will be evaluated on submission).

3. Navigating & Answering a Question:

  • Click on an option to select your answer. To deselect your chosen answer, click the Clear button at the bottom.
  • Use Next and Previous to move between questions sequentially, or click any question number in the scrollable right-hand Question Palette.
  • Use Mark for Review & Next to flag the current question for review and advance to the next question.
  • Upon submission, your score will be displayed along with detailed solutions/answers exclusively for incorrect and un-attempted questions.
