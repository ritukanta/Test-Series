Mock Test: General Instructions

Please read the following instructions carefully before starting the exam.

General Examination Rules & Scheme

  • Total Duration: 18 Minutes (Strictly calculated as 36 seconds per question for 30 questions).
  • Total Number of Questions: 30 Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs).
  • Marking Scheme:
    • +2.0 Marks for each correct response.
    • -0.50 Marks penalty for each incorrect response (Negative Marking).
    • 0.00 Marks for unattempted questions.
  • The countdown timer at the top right of the screen will show the remaining time available for you to complete the examination. When the timer reaches zero, the examination will end by itself and auto-submit.

Question Palette Color Codes (TCS / Eduquity Standard)

The Question Palette displayed on the right side of the screen will show the status of each question using one of the following symbols:

1 Not Visited: You have not visited the question yet.
2 Not Answered: You have visited the question, but not answered it.
3 Answered: You have answered the question.
4 Marked for Review: You have NOT answered the question, but have marked it for review.
5 Answered & Marked for Review: You have answered the question and marked it for review. (This will be considered for evaluation).

Navigating to a Question

  1. Click on the question number in the Question Palette to go directly to that question.
  2. Click on Next to save your answer for the current question and then move to the next question.
  3. Click on Previous to go back to the previous question.
  4. Click on Mark for Review & Next to save your answer for the current question (if answered), mark it for review, and then go to the next question.
  5. Click on Clear Response to deselect the chosen option.

Important Notice

  • Fullscreen mode will be requested upon clicking 'I am ready to begin'. You can exit fullscreen at any time using the 'Exit Fullscreen' button.
  • You can pause the test using the 'Pause' button if needed.
  • Solutions & Answer Key: Per exam guidelines, detailed solutions will be provided ONLY for incorrect and unattempted questions upon submission for targeted revision.
