General Exam Instructions (Please Read Carefully):

  1. The clock will be set at the server. The countdown timer in the top right corner of screen will display the remaining time available for you to complete the examination (59 minutes for 59 questions). When the timer reaches zero, the examination will end and submit automatically.
  2. One MCQ is displayed on the screen at a time with four distractor options. Options are randomized on each test refresh, while questions remain in their exact sequential order.
  3. No answer or hint is revealed during the test upon selecting an option. Detailed scores and solutions for incorrect and un-attempted questions are provided upon final submission.
  4. Clicking on an option selects it, but does not automatically jump to the next question. Use Next, Previous, or Mark for Review & Next to navigate.
  5. Upon clicking Start Mock Test, the exam interface will enter Full Screen Mode automatically. You may exit full screen anytime using the Exit Fullscreen button in the header.

Question Palette Symbols (Right Panel on Desktop):

1 You have not visited the question yet.
2 You have not answered the question.
3 You have answered the question.
4 You have NOT answered the question, but have marked it for review.
5 You have answered the question and marked it for review (will be evaluated on submission).
