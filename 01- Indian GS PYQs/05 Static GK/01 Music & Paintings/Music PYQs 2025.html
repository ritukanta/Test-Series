1. Test Rules & Navigation
  • The countdown timer in the top right corner displays the remaining time available for you to complete the examination. When the timer reaches zero, the examination will submit automatically.
  • Only one question is displayed on the screen at a time. The four distractor options are randomized on each session/refresh while keeping the question sequence fixed.
  • Selecting an option does not jump to the next question automatically or reveal answers during the test. Click Next (Save & Next) or Mark for Review & Next to record your status and move forward.
  • Clicking I am ready to begin will automatically launch the exam in Fullscreen mode. You can exit fullscreen anytime using the Exit Fullscreen button.
  • Upon submission, your detailed scorecard and solutions/explanations for incorrect and un-attempted questions will be displayed for analysis.
