General Instructions (Please Read Carefully)

  1. Strict Countdown Timer: The clock is set strictly at 36 seconds per question (for example, a 25-question test has a 15-minute timer). The countdown timer in the top-right corner displays the remaining time available for you to complete the examination. When the timer reaches zero, the examination will submit automatically.
  2. Question & Option Ordering: Questions are presented one at a time in their fixed sequential order (questions are not randomized on refresh). However, the four distractor options (A, B, C, D) are freshly randomized on every test load/refresh.
  3. No Mid-Test Hints or Answer Reveal: Selecting an option records your response silently without revealing correctness or hints during the test. Navigation to the next question is manual via the bottom control buttons (the interface never jumps automatically on option click).
  4. Marking Scheme & Post-Submission Solution Policy: Every correct answer awards +2 marks, and every wrong answer deducts 0.5 marks (-0.5 negative marking). No marks are deducted for unattempted questions. Upon submission, detailed solutions and explanations are displayed strictly for Incorrect & Unattempted questions.
  5. Question Number Palette Symbols: The Question Palette displayed on the right side of the screen (hidden on mobile screens) shows the status of each question using standard TCS iON / Eduquity indicators:
1 You have not visited the question yet.
2 You have not answered the question.
3 You have answered the question.
4 You have NOT answered the question, but have marked it for review.
5 The question(s) "Answered and Marked for Review" will be considered for evaluation.
