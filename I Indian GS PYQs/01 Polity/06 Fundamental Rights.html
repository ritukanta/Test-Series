Navigating & Answering a Question:
  1. Click on any option to select your answer. Selecting an option does not automatically jump to the next question or reveal the answer during the test.
  2. Use Next to save your current response and move to the next question, or Previous to move to the preceding question.
  3. Use Mark for Review & Next to flag a question for later review and advance to the next question.
  4. Use Clear to deselect your chosen option for the current question.
  5. The test will automatically enter Full Screen mode upon starting. You may exit full screen at any time using the Exit Fullscreen button in the top bar, or pause the timer using the Pause button.
  6. Upon final submission, your score analysis will be displayed along with detailed solutions strictly for incorrect and un-attempted questions.
