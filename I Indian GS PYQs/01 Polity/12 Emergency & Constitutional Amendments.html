General Instructions

  1. The total duration of this mock examination is 50 minutes for 50 questions (1 minute per question).
  2. The countdown timer in the top right corner of the screen displays the remaining time available for you to complete the examination. When the timer reaches zero, the examination will submit automatically.
  3. One Multiple Choice Question (MCQ) is displayed on the screen at a time with four options. Options are randomized on every load while the question sequence remains fixed.
  4. Selecting an option does not lock or reveal the answer during the test and does not automatically jump to the next question.
  5. The Question Palette displayed on the right side of the screen (on desktop/tablet) shows the status of each question using the following standard symbols:
1 You have not visited the question yet.
2 You have not answered the question.
3 You have answered the question.
4 You have NOT answered the question, but have marked it for review.
5 You have answered the question and marked it for review (will be evaluated for scoring).

Navigating & Answering a Question

  • Click on any option to select your response. To deselect your chosen response, click on the Clear button.
  • Click on Next to save your current response and move to the next question.
  • Click on Previous to navigate back to the preceding question.
  • Click on Mark for Review & Next to mark the current question for review and proceed to the next question.
  • Use the Pause button if you need to pause the countdown timer, Exit Fullscreen to leave full-screen mode, and Submit to finish the test and view your score and solutions for incorrect and un-attempted questions.
