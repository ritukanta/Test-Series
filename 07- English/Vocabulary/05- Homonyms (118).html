General Instructions

Default Language:
Notice: Please note all questions will appear one-by-one. When you click "I am ready to begin", the test will enter fullscreen mode and the timer will start.

A. General Information:

  1. The total duration of this mock test is 15 minutes.
  2. The server clock is displayed at the top right corner of the screen and will count down to 00:00. Once the timer reaches zero, the test will submit automatically.
  3. Each question carries +2.00 marks for a correct answer and -0.50 negative marks for an incorrect answer. Unattempted questions carry 0 marks.

B. Navigating to a Question:

  • Click on the question number in the Question Palette on the right of your screen to go to that numbered question directly.
  • Click on Save & Next to save your answer for the current question and then go to the next question.
  • Click on Mark for Review & Next to save your answer (or leave unselected) and mark it for review, then proceed to the next question.

C. Question Palette Symbols & Color Codes:

1 You have not visited the question yet.
2 You have not answered the question.
3 You have answered the question.
4 You have NOT answered the question, but have marked the question for review.
5
The question(s) "Answered and Marked for Review" will be considered in evaluation.

D. Answering Questions:

  • To select your answer, click on the button of one of the options.
  • To deselect your chosen answer, click on the Clear Response button.
  • To change your chosen answer, click on the button of another option.
  • To save your answer, you MUST click on the Save & Next button.
