Please read the following instructions carefully before starting the examination:

  1. The clock will be set at the server. The countdown timer in the top right corner of the screen will display the remaining time available for you to complete the examination. Move through the questions steadily; when the timer reaches zero, the examination will end and submit by itself automatically.
  2. The Question Palette displayed on the right side of the screen (on desktop/tablet viewports) will show the status of each question using one of the following standard TCS / Eduquity symbols:
Symbol Status Meaning
1 You have NOT visited the question yet.
2 You have NOT answered the question.
3 You have Answered the question.
4 You have NOT answered the question, but have marked the question for review.
5 The question(s) "Answered and Marked for Review" will be considered for evaluation.

Navigating & Answering a Question:

  • Click on the question number in the Question Palette at the right of your screen to go to that numbered question directly. Note that using this option does NOT save your answer to the current question unless you have selected an option.
  • To select your answer, click on one of the four option rows. Clicking an option does not automatically jump to the next question and does not display any hints or answer validation during the exam.
  • Click on Next to save your current selection and move to the next question, or Previous to move to the preceding question.
  • Click on Clear to deselect your chosen answer for the current question.
  • Click on Mark for Review & Next to mark the current question for review and advance to the next question.
  • Upon clicking Start Mock Test, the interface will automatically enter Fullscreen Mode. You can exit fullscreen at any time using the Exit Fullscreen button on the top bar, or pause the timer using the Pause button.
  • Upon final submission, your score analysis will be displayed along with detailed solutions strictly for incorrect and un-attempted questions.
