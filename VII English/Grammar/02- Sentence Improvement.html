General Instructions — Please Read Carefully

Questions: 25 MCQs Duration: 15 Minutes Correct: +2.00 Incorrect: -0.25

1. Examination Rules & Timer:

  • Total duration of this mock test is 15 minutes (900 seconds). The countdown timer in the top-right corner displays the remaining time.
  • When the timer reaches zero, the examination will submit automatically without requiring any action.
  • Each correct answer awards +2.00 marks, and each wrong answer deducts 0.25 marks. Un-attempted questions carry 0 marks.
  • Questions appear in fixed order (Q.1 to Q.25), while the four options (A, B, C, D) are randomized on every test launch/refresh.
  • Selecting an option does not reveal the answer during the test and does not automatically jump to the next question.
  • Upon starting the test, the browser will automatically enter Fullscreen Mode. You can exit fullscreen or pause the test anytime using the top control buttons.

2. Question Palette Symbols (Right Panel on Desktop):

1 You have not visited the question yet.
2 You have visited but not answered the question.
3 You have answered the question.
4 You have NOT answered the question, but marked it for review.
5 You have answered the question and marked it for review (will be evaluated for marks on submission).

3. Navigating & Answering a Question:

  • Click on a radio option to select your response. To deselect your chosen response, click the Clear button.
  • Click Next or Previous to navigate between questions, or click any question number in the scrollable Question Palette on the right.
  • Click Mark for Review & Next to flag the current question for review and advance to the next question.
  • After submission, detailed grammatical solutions are provided strictly for incorrect and un-attempted questions for focused revision.
