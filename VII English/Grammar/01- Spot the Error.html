Please read the instructions carefully before starting the examination:

  1. This mock test consists of 25 Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs) from the selected chapter. Each question has 4 options out of which only one is correct.
  2. The total duration of this test is 15 minutes. The countdown timer in the top right corner of the screen displays the remaining time available for you to complete the examination. When the timer reaches zero (00:00), the examination will submit automatically.
  3. Marking Scheme: You will be awarded +2.00 marks for each correct response, and -0.25 marks will be deducted for each wrong response. No marks (0.00) will be awarded or deducted for un-attempted questions.
  4. Question Navigation & Behaviour: One question is displayed on the screen at a time. Selecting an option does not automatically advance to the next question or reveal the answer during the test. Use the Next, Previous, Clear, or Mark for Review & Next buttons at the bottom of the screen.
  5. The Question Palette displayed on the right side of the screen (on desktop/tablet) will show the status of each question using the following standard symbols:
1 You have not visited the question yet.
2 You have not answered the question.
3 You have answered the question.
4 You have NOT answered the question, but have marked it for review.
5 You have answered the question and marked it for review (will be evaluated for marks on submission).
  • Upon clicking Start Mock Test, the exam interface will automatically enter Fullscreen Mode. You can exit fullscreen anytime using the Exit Fullscreen button.
  • After submission, your score analysis will be displayed along with detailed solutions/answers exclusively for incorrect and un-attempted questions.
