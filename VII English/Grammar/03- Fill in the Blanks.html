Candidate Information: Candidate Roll: 2025-CGL-0912 | Subject: General English (Grammar) | Total Marks: 50.00

General Instructions:

  1. Total duration of the examination is 15 minutes.
  2. The clock will be set at the server. The countdown timer in the top right corner of the screen will display the remaining time available for you to complete the examination.
  3. When the timer reaches zero, the examination will end automatically. You are not required to end or submit your examination once the timer ends.
  4. Each question carries +2.00 marks for a correct answer.
  5. There is a negative marking of -0.25 marks for each wrong answer. No marks are deducted for un-attempted questions.

Question Palette Legend:

The Question Palette displayed on the right side of screen will show the status of each question using one of the following symbols:

1 You have not visited the question yet.
2 You have visited but not answered the question.
3 You have answered the question.
4 You have NOT answered the question, but have marked the question for review.
5 You have answered the question, and marked it for review. This will be considered for evaluation.

Navigating & Answering Questions:

  • Click on the question number in the Question Palette to go to that question directly.
  • To select your answer, click on the option button. Clicking an option does not advance to the next question automatically.
  • Click Save & Next to save your answer and move to the next question.
  • Click Mark for Review & Next to mark it for review and move to the next question.
  • Click Clear Response to deselect your chosen answer.
