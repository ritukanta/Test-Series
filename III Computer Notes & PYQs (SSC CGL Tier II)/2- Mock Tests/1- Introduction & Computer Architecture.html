General Exam Instructions:

  1. The clock has been set at the server and the countdown timer at the top right corner of your screen will display the time remaining for you to complete the exam. Each question carries 1 minute of time allocation.
  2. When the countdown timer reaches zero, the examination will submit automatically without requiring you to click the Submit button.
  3. The test will automatically enter Full Screen Mode when you begin. You can exit full screen anytime using the Exit Fullscreen button on the top bar.
  4. One Multiple Choice Question (MCQ) with four options is displayed at a time. Selecting an option does not automatically jump to the next question or reveal the answer during the test.
  5. Use the Next, Previous, Clear, and Mark for Review & Next buttons at the bottom of the screen to navigate and manage your responses.
  6. On submission, your score analysis will be displayed along with detailed solutions strictly for incorrect and un-attempted questions.
