Welcome to the Computer Based Examination ().

1. Test Duration & Structure

  • Total Number of Questions: 0 Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs) with 4 options each.
  • Total Time Allotted: 0 Minutes (Strict countdown timer).
  • When the countdown timer reaches zero (00:00), the examination will submit automatically.
  • Questions appear in a fixed sequence, while the 4 options for each question are randomized on every fresh session.
  • Selecting an option does not lock or reveal the answer during the test and does not jump to the next question automatically.

2. Question Number Palette Symbols

The Question Palette displayed on the right side of the screen (on desktop/tablet views) shows the status of each question using the following standard symbols:

Symbol Meaning
1 You have not visited the question yet.
2 You have not answered the question.
3 You have answered the question.
4 You have NOT answered the question, but have marked it for review.
5 You have answered the question and marked it for review (will be evaluated for scoring).

3. Navigating & Answering a Question

  1. Click on any question number in the scrollable Question Palette on the right to jump directly to that question (note: clicking a palette number does not save your current selection unless you click Next or Mark for Review & Next, or simply select an option which is preserved in memory).
  2. Click on Next to save your selected response and move to the next question.
  3. Click on Previous to move back to the preceding question.
  4. Click on Mark for Review & Next to mark the current question for review and move to the next question.
  5. Click on Clear to deselect your chosen option for the current question.
  6. The test enters Fullscreen automatically upon starting. You can exit fullscreen at any time using the Exit Fullscreen button on the top bar.
  7. Upon submission, your score analysis will be displayed along with detailed solutions/answers exclusively for incorrect and un-attempted questions.
