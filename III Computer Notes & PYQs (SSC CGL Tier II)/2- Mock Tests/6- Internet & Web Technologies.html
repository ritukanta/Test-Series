General Exam Instructions:
  1. The clock is set strictly according to the number of questions in the mock test (1 minute per question). The countdown timer in the top right corner displays the remaining time available for you to complete the examination. When the timer reaches zero, the examination will submit automatically.
  2. The Question Palette displayed on the right side of the screen (hidden on mobile phone screens) shows the status of each question using the following standard symbols:
Symbol Status Meaning
1 You have not visited the question yet.
2 You have not answered the question (visited but no option selected).
3 You have answered the question.
4 You have NOT answered the question, but have marked it for review.
5 You have answered the question AND marked it for review (will be evaluated on submission).
Navigating & Answering a Question:
  1. Click on an option to select your answer. Selecting an option does not automatically jump to the next question and does not reveal the answer during the test.
  2. Use Next and Previous to move between questions, Clear to deselect your chosen option for the current question, and Mark for Review & Next to flag a question for review and move to the next question.
  3. Use the independently scrollable Question Palette on the right to jump directly to any question number without scrolling the main exam interface.
  4. Upon clicking Begin Test, the exam will automatically enter Fullscreen Mode (you may exit fullscreen at any time using the Exit Fullscreen button).
