02. Important Days: Article 370, Handloom & Independence Day
00:01:51
MCQ 1 Who among the following drafted Article 370 of the Indian Constitution?
Correct Answer: N. Gopalaswami Ayyangar
  • Historical Background: Article 370 conferred temporary special autonomous status to Jammu & Kashmir. It was drafted in 1949 by N. Gopalaswami Ayyangar, who served as Prime Minister of Jammu & Kashmir (1937–1943) and later as a Union Cabinet Minister.
  • Abrogation Milestone: On 5th August 2019, the President of India issued Constitutional Orders (CO 272 & CO 273), and Parliament passed the Jammu and Kashmir Reorganisation Act, 2019, effectively revoking the special status and bifurcating the state into two Union Territories: Jammu & Kashmir (with a legislature) and Ladakh (without a legislature).
  • Significance in August: 5th August marks the completion of 5 full years of the abrogation of Article 370.
Static GK: Drafting Committee of the Constituent Assembly
  • Constitution of Drafting Committee: Set up on 29th August 1947 with 7 members.
  • Chairman: Dr. B.R. Ambedkar (Father of the Indian Constitution).
  • Members: N. Gopalaswami Ayyangar, Alladi Krishnaswami Iyer, Dr. K.M. Munshi, Syed Mohammad Saadulla, N. Madhava Rau (replaced B.L. Mitter), T.T. Krishnamachari (replaced D.P. Khaitan).
National Handloom Day (August 7)
  • Historical Genesis: Commemorates the formal launch of the Swadeshi Movement on 7th August 1905 at the Calcutta Town Hall to protest the Partition of Bengal by Lord Curzon.
  • Significance: Swadeshi encouraged indigenous Indian textiles and boycott of Manchester cotton. Handloom represents the soul of rural Indian manufacturing.
  • Inaugural Edition: First proclaimed by Prime Minister Narendra Modi in Chennai on 7th August 2015. 2024 marks the 10th edition.
  • Nodal Ministry: Ministry of Textiles, Government of India.
78th Independence Day (August 15)
  • Edition & Milestone: India celebrated its 78th Independence Day on August 15, 2024.
  • Official Theme: "Viksit Bharat @ 2047" (Transforming India into a fully developed nation by the centenary year of independence in 2047).
  • National Address: Address by Prime Minister Narendra Modi from the ramparts of the Red Fort in New Delhi, emphasizing indigenous manufacturing, critical technologies, and green transition.
03. ISRO Facts & Pioneer Space Missions (Static GK Deep Dive)
00:12:00
ISRO Institutional Profile & Landmark Space Missions
  • Formation: Founded on 15 August 1969, superseding INCOSPAR (Indian National Committee for Space Research, established by Dr. Vikram Sarabhai in 1962).
  • Headquarters: Antariksh Bhavan, Bengaluru, Karnataka.
  • Current Chairman: S. Somanath (Secretary, Department of Space).
  • Primary Launch Center: Satish Dhawan Space Centre (SDSC) SHAR, Sriharikota, Tirupati district, Andhra Pradesh.
  • Key Propulsion Lab: Liquid Propulsion Systems Centre (LPSC) at Valiamala (Thiruvananthapuram) and Bengaluru.
  • Aryabhata (1975): India's first satellite, launched on 19 April 1975 using a Soviet Kosmos-3M launch vehicle from Kapustin Yar.
  • Chandrayaan-1 (2008): Launched 22 October 2008 aboard PSLV-C11. Confirmed the presence of water molecules ($H_2O/OH$) on lunar soil. Moon Impact Probe (MIP) landed near Shackleton Crater.
  • Mars Orbiter Mission / MOM (2013): Launched 5 November 2013 on PSLV-C25; entered Martian orbit on 24 September 2014. India became the 1st Asian nation to reach Mars and 1st globally on maiden attempt.
  • Chandrayaan-3 (2023): Launched 14 July 2023 on LVM3-M4; successfully made soft-landing near the Lunar South Pole at Shiv Shakti Point on 23 August 2023.
  • Aditya-L1 (2023): India's first dedicated solar observatory; successfully inserted into halo orbit around Sun-Earth L1 point on 6 January 2024.

GAURAV — Long-Range Glide Bomb (LRGB)
  • Developer: Research Centre Imarat (RCI), DRDO, Hyderabad.
  • Successful Test: Maiden flight trial conducted successfully from an Indian Air Force Su-30 MKI fighter jet off the coast of Odisha.
  • Specifications: 1,000-kg class winged precision-guided glide bomb designed to destroy enemy airfields, bunkers, and command centers from standoff distances.
  • Guidance: Highly sophisticated Inertial Navigation System integrated with GPS/NavIC satellite positioning.
Exercise Tarang Shakti 2024
  • Distinction: Indian Air Force's first-ever multinational air combat exercise held on Indian soil.
  • Two Distinct Phases:
    • Phase 1 (Sulur, Tamil Nadu): 6–14 August 2024. Participants included Germany (Eurofighter Typhoon), France (Rafale), Spain, and UK.
    • Phase 2 (Jodhpur, Rajasthan): 30 August – 14 September 2024. Participants included USA, Australia, Greece (first deployment in India), Japan, Singapore, UAE.
  • Aircraft Deployed: Over 150 aircraft, including LCA Tejas, Su-30 MKI, Rafale, Mirage 2000, and airborne warning systems.
Exercise Udara Shakti & Mitra Shakti 2024
  • Ex Udara Shakti 2024: Bilateral air combat exercise conducted between Indian Air Force (IAF Su-30 MKI) and Royal Malaysian Air Force (RMAF) at Kuantan Air Base, Malaysia.
  • Ex Mitra Shakti 2024 (10th Edition): Joint military counter-insurgency exercise between Indian Army (Rajputana Rifles) and Sri Lankan Army held at Army Training School, Maduru Oya, Sri Lanka.
INS Arighat — India's 2nd Nuclear Submarine
  • Commissioning: Formally commissioned into the Indian Navy on 29 August 2024 at Visakhapatnam in the presence of Defence Minister Rajnath Singh.
  • Class & Role: India's second indigenous nuclear-powered ballistic missile submarine (SSBN) built under the Advanced Technology Vessel (ATV) Project, succeeding INS Arihant (commissioned 2016).
  • Armament: Armed with nuclear-tipped K-15 (Sagarika) Submarine-Launched Ballistic Missiles (SLBMs) with a strike range of 750 km. Solidifies India's nuclear triad and "No First Use" deterrence.

BHISHM Portable Cube (Project Aarogya Maitri)
  • Disaster Deployment: Deployed by the Indian Air Force and Army in Meppadi/Chooralmala, Wayanad (Kerala) following devastating landslides.
  • What is BHISHM: Bharat Health Initiative for Sahyog Hita and Maitri. The world's first portable, modular emergency hospital.
  • Capacity: Comprises 72 lightweight mini-cubes that can be air-dropped by drone or helicopter, unpacking into a fully operational field hospital with operating theatre, X-ray, ultrasound, ICU ventilator, power generator, and mini-lab able to treat up to 200 trauma patients within 12 minutes.
Nabhmitra Satellite Communication System
  • Developer: ISRO's Space Applications Centre (SAC), Ahmedabad.
  • Function: Two-way satellite communication transponder installed on coastal marine fishing vessels.
  • Features: Relays real-time cyclone warnings, potential fishing zone (PFZ) data, and distress alerts in local regional languages directly to maritime control stations.
SSLV-D3 Launch / EOS-08 Satellite
  • Milestone: ISRO successfully completed the third and final developmental flight (D3) of the Small Satellite Launch Vehicle (SSLV) on 16 August 2024 from Sriharikota.
  • Payload: Placed primary payload EOS-08 (Earth Observation Satellite-08) and passenger cubesat SR-0 DEMOSAT into an orbit of 475 km.
  • Operational Transition: With this success, SSLV developmental phase is complete; technology transfer to private industry will proceed.
Asha — High-Protein Pigeon Pea Genome Assembly
  • Institution: ICAR-National Institute for Plant Biotechnology (NIPB), New Delhi.
  • Discovery: Completed chromosome-level reference genome assembly of the popular Arhar/Tur variety named "Asha" (ICPL 87119).
  • Impact: Accelerates genetic breeding of climate-resilient, disease-resistant, and high-yield pulse varieties to boost domestic pulse production.
WHO Mpox Global Health Emergency (PHEIC)
  • Declaration: On 14 August 2024, WHO Director-General Dr. Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus declared the rapid spread of Mpox a Public Health Emergency of International Concern (PHEIC).
  • Epicenter & Strain: Outbreak driven by the virulent Clade 1b strain originating in the Democratic Republic of the Congo (DRC) and spilling into Burundi, Rwanda, and Uganda.
World's First mRNA Lung Cancer Vaccine Trial
  • Vaccine Candidate: BNT116, manufactured by BioNTech.
  • Clinical Trial: Phase 1 clinical trial launched across 34 research sites in seven countries (UK, US, Germany, Hungary, Poland, Spain, Turkey).
  • Mechanism: Targets non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) by instructing the immune system to recognize tumor markers without harming healthy tissue.

Unified Pension Scheme (UPS) — Approved by Union Cabinet
Approved on 24 August 2024 by PM Narendra Modi-led Cabinet; operational from 1 April 2025 for 23 lakh central employees.
Pillar Benefit Formula Qualifying Criteria
1. Assured Pension 50% of the average basic pay drawn over the last 12 months prior to superannuation. Minimum 25 years of qualifying service (proportionate pension for service between 10 and 25 years).
2. Assured Family Pension 60% of the pension drawn by the employee immediately before demise. Applicable to spouse/dependents upon employee's demise.
3. Assured Minimum Pension ₹10,000 per month upon superannuation. Minimum qualifying service of 10 years.
4. Inflation Indexation Dearness Relief (DR) calculated in accordance with All India Consumer Price Index for Industrial Workers (AICPI-IW). Applicable to Assured Pension, Family Pension, and Minimum Pension.
5. Govt Contribution Hike Central Government contribution increased from 14% to 18.5% of basic pay. Employee contribution remains unchanged at 10%. Provides funded sovereign assurance without market-linked downside.
PM Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY) Completes 10 Years
  • Milestone: Completed 10 full years on 28th August 2024 (Launched 28 August 2014).

BioE3 Policy & Vigyan Dhara Scheme
