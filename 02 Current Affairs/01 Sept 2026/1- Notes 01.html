1A. Deep-Dive Featured Important Days & Static Linkages (As Taught First in Lecture)

11 September Environment History

1. National Forest Martyrs' Day (राष्ट्रीय वन शहीद दिवस)

  • Observed On: 11 September annually across India.
  • Instituted By: Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC) in 2013 to honour forest personnel and citizens who laid down their lives protecting forests and wildlife.
  • Historical Event Commemorated: The Khejarli Massacre (1730 AD) in Khejarli village, Marwar Kingdom (Jodhpur, Rajasthan).
  • Leader & Community: Led by Amrita Devi Bishnoi, who sacrificed her life along with her three daughters (Asu, Ratni, Bhagu) and 363 members of the Bishnoi community on the orders of Maharaja Abhai Singh's minister Giridhar Bhandari to protect sacred Khejri trees (Prosopis cineraria — State Tree of Rajasthan & Telangana).
  • Exam Distinction (Khejarli vs. Chipko Movement):
    • Khejarli Massacre: Took place in 1730 in Rajasthan (inspired the Chipko Movement).
    • Chipko Movement: Took place in 1973 in Chamoli district, Uttarakhand (then UP), led by Sunderlal Bahuguna, Chandi Prasad Bhatt, and Gaura Devi.
💡 Memory Trick (As Taught): Draw two tree trunks side-by-side (1 1) → 11 September is National Forest Martyrs' Day.
14 September Polity & Constitutional GK

2. Hindi Diwas (हिंदी दिवस) vs. World Hindi Day

  • National Hindi Diwas: Observed on 14 September every year.
  • Historical Context: On 14 September 1949, the Constituent Assembly of India officially adopted Hindi written in the Devanagari script as the Official Language of the Union of India (while retaining the international form of Indian numerals). First celebrated in 1953.
  • Constitutional & Legal Provisions:
    • Article 343(1): Declares Hindi in Devanagari script and English as the 2 Official Languages of the Union Government. (Note: India has NO National Language and NO National Sport).
    • Official Languages Act, 1963: Enacted to continue the use of English for official purposes alongside Hindi beyond the initial 15-year transition period (post-1965) following protests in South India.
    • Eighth Schedule (8th Schedule): Recognises 22 Scheduled Languages (including Hindi; English is NOT in the 8th Schedule).
  • World Hindi Day (विश्व हिंदी दिवस): Observed on 10 January (commemorating the 1st World Hindi Conference held in Nagpur on 10 January 1975).
16 September Science & Ecology

3. International Day for the Preservation of the Ozone Layer (World Ozone Day)

  • Observed On: 16 September (proclaimed by UNGA in 1994).
  • 2026 Theme: "Global Action for a Cooler Planet" (UNEP Ozone Secretariat — marking 10 years of the Kigali Amendment).
  • Scientific & Atmospheric Context:
    • Molecular Formula: $\text{O}_3$ (Trioxygen).
    • Atmospheric Layer: Located in the Stratosphere (~15–35 km altitude), where it absorbs harmful solar Ultraviolet (UV-B) radiation. Unlike the Troposphere (where temperature decreases with height due to the normal lapse rate), temperature in the Stratosphere increases with altitude due to UV absorption by ozone.
  • Treaties & Amendments:
    • Vienna Convention (1985): Framework for ozone protection.
    • Montreal Protocol (Signed 16 Sept 1987): Legally binding treaty to phase out Ozone Depleting Substances (ODS) such as CFCs (Chlorofluorocarbons), Halons, and Carbon Tetrachloride ($\text{CCl}_4$).
    • Kigali Amendment (2016, Rwanda): Phase-down of HFCs (Hydrofluorocarbons) — potent greenhouse gases that warm the planet.
