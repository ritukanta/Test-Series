General Instructions & Examination Guidelines

Please read the following instructions carefully before proceeding to start the examination:

  1. The countdown timer at the top-right of your screen will display the remaining time available for you to complete the examination. When the timer reaches 00:00:00, the examination will submit automatically.
  2. Each question consists of Four Multiple Choice Distractor Options. On each page reload/refresh, the four options are randomised automatically to prevent pattern bias.
  3. Only one question is displayed on the screen at any time. Selecting an option will NOT advance to the next question automatically; you must manually click Next or Mark for Review & Next.
  4. No answers, scores, or solutions are displayed during the test. Score analysis and detailed solutions are provided strictly upon final submission.

Question Palette Symbols & Status Legend

The Question Palette displayed on the right-side of your screen uses the following status symbols:

1 Not Visited: You have not visited the question yet.
2 Not Answered: You have visited the question but have not answered it yet.
3 Answered: You have selected an answer for the question.
4 Marked for Review: You have not answered the question, but have marked it for review.
5 Answered & Marked for Review: You have answered the question, but marked it for review.

Navigating & Answering Questions

  • To Answer: Click on the desired option's radio button.
  • To Deselect / Clear: Click on the Clear button to deselect your chosen answer for the active question.
  • To Save & Advance: Click Next to proceed to the subsequent question.
  • To Mark for Review: Click Mark for Review & Next to save your response (if any) and flag the question for subsequent reconsideration.
  • Direct Navigation: You may click any question number on the right-side Question Palette to jump directly to that question.
  • Fullscreen Mode: The test interface enters fullscreen mode automatically upon beginning. You may use the Exit Fullscreen button at any time.
