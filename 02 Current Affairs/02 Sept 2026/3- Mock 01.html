General Examination Instructions

  1. The clock will be set at the server. The countdown timer at the top right corner displays the remaining time available for you to complete the examination.
  2. When the timer reaches zero, the examination will end automatically. You will not be required to end or submit your examination.
  3. The Question Palette displayed on the right side of the screen will show the status of each question using one of the following symbols:
1 Not Visited: You have not visited the question yet.
2 Not Answered: You have visited the question but have not answered it.
3 Answered: You have answered the question.
4 Marked for Review: You have not answered the question, but have marked it for review.
5 Answered & Marked for Review: The question has been answered as well as marked for review (will be evaluated).

Navigating and Answering Questions

  • Click on an option radio button to select your answer.
  • To deselect your chosen answer, click on the Clear Response button.
  • To save your answer and move to the next question, click on the Save & Next button.
  • To mark the question for review, click on the Mark for Review & Next button.
  • You may navigate between questions using the question number palette on the right side.
